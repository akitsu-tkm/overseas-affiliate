# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: {{ site.time | date: "%B %-d, %Y" }}_

StackScout ("we", "us", "our") publishes this site at stackscout.com. This
policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect

StackScout is a static site with no user accounts, comments, or forms. We do
not collect personal information directly. If you email us, we retain that
correspondence only to respond to you.

If we add analytics or an email newsletter in the future, this policy will
be updated to describe what's collected and why before that feature goes
live.

## Cookies

This site does not set first-party tracking cookies. Third-party sites you
reach through our affiliate links may set their own cookies to track
referrals — that's governed by their own privacy policies, not ours.

## Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. See our
[affiliate disclosure]({{ '/affiliate-disclosure/' | relative_url }}) for
details. Clicking an affiliate link and signing up on the destination site
is subject to that site's own privacy practices.

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves (for example, if we add
analytics or a mailing list). Material changes will be reflected on this
page with an updated "last updated" date.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at hello@stackscout.com.
