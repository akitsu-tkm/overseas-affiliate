# Affiliate Disclosure

StackScout is reader-supported. Some of the links on this site — including
links in our reviews and any "Try [Product]" buttons — are affiliate links.
If you click through and sign up for a product, we may earn a commission
from the company being reviewed, at **no additional cost to you**.

This is how we keep StackScout free to read and independent of any single
advertiser.

## What this does — and doesn't — mean for our reviews

- We only join affiliate programs for products we've genuinely evaluated and
  would recommend regardless of compensation.
- Affiliate relationships do not determine our ratings, rankings, or
  conclusions. A product does not get a better review because it pays a
  higher commission.
- We do not accept payment in exchange for a positive review.
- Prices, features, and terms mentioned in our reviews can change after
  publication — always confirm current details on the vendor's own site
  before purchasing.

## Where this applies

Any outbound link to a product we review may be an affiliate link. Links to
affiliate programs are marked with `rel="sponsored"` in the page's HTML, in
line with search engine guidelines on disclosing paid or affiliate links.

This disclosure is provided in accordance with the U.S. Federal Trade
Commission's (FTC) guidelines on endorsements and testimonials, 16 CFR Part
255.
