# About StaySaaS

StaySaaS reviews SaaS tools across project management, design, security,
online learning, and marketing — with a focus on software that's still worth
paying for after the first year, not just at signup.

We look for tools with genuine long-term value: reasonable pricing, a real
free trial, and a track record that holds up over time. Every review is
based on hands-on use of the product's free trial or public documentation,
and reflects our own opinion, not the vendor's marketing copy.

Some of the links on this site are affiliate links — see our
[affiliate disclosure]({{ '/affiliate-disclosure/' | relative_url }}) for
details on how that works and how it does (and doesn't) affect what we
recommend.
