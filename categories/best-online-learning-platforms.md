Every learning platform claims to teach you something new, but they're built on very different models, and that difference matters more than the course catalog.

**Structured course vs. habit-building.** Some platforms are built like a curriculum with a defined start and end. Others are designed around short, frequent sessions meant to build a daily habit. Neither is better — it depends on how you actually learn.

**What the free tier really includes.** This varies enormously across the category, from a single free lesson to a genuinely complete free product. Don't assume "free trial" means the same thing on every platform.

**Renewal pricing, not just the signup price.** Some of the steepest price jumps in software happen here, between an introductory rate and what you're actually billed after the first term. Always check the second-year number.
