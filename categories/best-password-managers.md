Security software is one of the few software categories where the wrong choice doesn't just waste money — it creates risk. A few things matter more than the marketing page suggests.

**What's actually encrypted, and where.** Look for zero-knowledge architecture, where even the company itself can't read your data, and confirm whether that applies to every plan tier or just the higher-priced ones.

**Cross-device and family/team support.** A password manager or VPN you only trust on one device isn't very useful. Check how many devices and platforms are covered before you're locked into a plan.

**What happens if you leave.** Exporting your data cleanly — passwords, secure notes, connection logs — matters more here than in almost any other software category. Check this before you commit, not after.
